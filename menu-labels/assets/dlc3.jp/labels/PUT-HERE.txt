Labels for language: Japanese
=============================

Twelve files, one file per label. The names must be exactly these:

    mode.png       モード                       (control mode; English: MODE, Russian for cross-check: «РЕЖИМ»)
    stick type.png スティック種類                   (stick type; English: STICK TYPE, Russian for cross-check: «ТИП СТИКА»)
    stick size.png スティック大きさ                  (stick size; English: STICK SCALE, Russian for cross-check: «РАЗМЕР СТИКА»)
    static.png     固定                        (the stick stays in place; English: STATIC, Russian for cross-check: «СТАТИЧНЫЙ»)
    dynamic.png    追従                        (the stick sits under the finger; English: DYNAMIC, Russian for cross-check: «ДИНАМИЧНЫЙ»)
    small.png      小                         (size; English: SMALL, Russian for cross-check: «МАЛЫЙ»)
    medium.png     中                         (size; English: MEDIUM, Russian for cross-check: «СРЕДНИЙ»)
    big.png        大                         (size; English: BIG, Russian for cross-check: «БОЛЬШОЙ»)
    analogue.png   アナログ                      (smooth control; English: ANALOGUE, Russian for cross-check: «АНАЛОГ»)
    digital.png    デジタル                      (eight directions; English: DIGITAL, Russian for cross-check: «ЦИФРОВОЙ»)
    aim lock.png   照準固定                      (native menu string; English: AIM LOCK, Russian for cross-check: «БЛОК СТРЕЛЬБЫ»)
    found hud.png  発見済み                      (native menu string; English: FOUND HUD, Russian for cross-check: «НАЙДЕННОЕ»)

The last two are native menu strings; in this language they are either
untranslated or only half translated. The owner redrew them in Russian;
do the same here.

HOW TO DRAW
-----------
  - Transparent background, letters only, no margins needed — I crop it
    myself.
  - NO anti-aliasing, hard pixels. Do not draw the edge: I add it by
    downscaling, the same way it was done for English and Russian.
  - Letter height ABOUT 22 PIXELS. Not two or three times the target
    size: at an integer multiple the edge does not form at all — this was
    checked by measurement.
  - Any dark colour is fine; I recolour it to the native ink (54,47,45)
    anyway.

I fit the width to the line myself. If a word turns out really long, I will
say so — the letters would come out lower than the native ones, and a
shorter word would be better.

TITLE SCREEN, separately
------------------------

lettering at the bottom is drawn TWICE — edit both. It currently reads
«PRESS START»; needed: DO NOT DRAW — copy over 「たっちしてすたーと」 from sample-japanese-title.png

Unlike the menu labels, the title is drawn in HARD PIXELS, with no
anti-aliasing and no downscaling: every native title has exactly zero
half-tones. If you hand it in anti-aliased, I will harden it with a
threshold.
