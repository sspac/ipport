WHAT NEEDS TO BE DRAWN, BY LANGUAGE
===================================

Two tasks: redraw the lettering on the title screen (5 files, Japanese is a
copy-over) and draw the lettering for our menu items (6 files, 10 labels each).

The tree here mirrors the game's. Edit the files in place without renaming
them — I then put the folder back with one command. The `sample-english`
folder holds the English files, already done.


TASK 1. TITLE SCREEN
====================

File:   <language>/gfx/ui/main menu/titlemenu_ios.png
Sheet:  480 x 540
Where:  the lettering at the bottom, drawn TWICE — two identical variants side
        by side, edit both. Above y=488 the sheet is the same for every
        language; do not touch the background.
Width:  each copy gets a 156-pixel cut-out. The English touch version takes
        141, the Russian «НАЖМИТЕ СТАРТ» (Russian "PRESS START") takes 149.
        So the margin is small: about 15-16 characters at the density the
        English artists used.

TASK 2. MENU ITEM LABELS
========================

File:  <language>/gfx/ui/main menu/optionsextra.png
Sheet: 256 x 512

The sheet is now twice as tall, and all our labels have moved to the bottom
half in a straight column. This was done because in the old spot the German
native labels ran right over our boxes, and there was not enough width for
long translations there anyway. Now the boxes are the same in every language
and roomy.

IMPORTANT: draw from the TOP LEFT corner of the box. The extra space to the
right and below is margin; it is transparent and not visible on screen. If you
shift the lettering inside the box, it shifts by exactly the same amount in
the game.

the English label in the same row.

What the values mean, in case a wording seems off: STATIC — the stick stays in
place, DYNAMIC — it appears under the finger. For Japanese and Chinese the
words were chosen by meaning: 固定 "fixed", 追従 and 跟随 "follows the finger".

How much fits. The native density is about 9 pixels per Cyrillic or Latin
letter, about 14 per CJK character. The boxes are deliberately roomy, but the
limit comes not from the box but from the paper:

    item names  — ink no longer than 90 pixels
    values      — ink no longer than 70 pixels

Any longer and the lettering runs off the edge of the paper sheet in the game.
Long translations such as «РАЗМЕР СТИКА» (Russian "STICK SIZE") or
«ДИНАМИЧНЫЙ» (Russian "DYNAMIC") will have to be drawn condensed; the English
artists do the same themselves — their STICK TYPE runs at 7.4 pixels per
character.

Lettering height — 12-15 pixels, as in the English sample.


HOW TO DRAW SO IT MATCHES THE NATIVE LETTERING
==============================================

Ink (54,47,45) at full opacity. The edge in the same shade at an alpha of
about 102, half-tones 15-19%.

Draw strictly at 100%. At any other zoom the image lands between texels and
shimmers when scrolling.
