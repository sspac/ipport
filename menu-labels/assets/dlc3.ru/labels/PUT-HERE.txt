The drawn labels go here, one file per label.

The file names must be exactly these (otherwise they are not picked up):

    MODE.png   STICK_TYPE.png   STICK_SCALE.png
    STATIC.png   DYNAMIC.png
    SMALL.png   MEDIUM.png   BIG.png
    ANALOGUE.png   DIGITAL.png

How to draw:

  - Transparent background, letters only.
  - NO anti-aliasing, hard pixels. Do not draw the edge — I add it
    myself by downscaling, the same way I did the English ones.
  - Letter height ABOUT 22 PIXELS. Not two or three times the target
    size: at an integer multiple the edge does not form at all, checked by
    measurement. Twenty-two is the proven size; the English labels were
    made from it.
  - Any dark colour is fine; I recolour it to the native ink (54,47,45)
    anyway.
  - No margins around the letters needed, I crop them myself.

Width after downscaling: up to 90 pixels for item names, up to 70 for
values. If it comes out wider, I will say so — the lettering would run off
the edge of the paper sheet in the game.
