The last two are native menu strings; in this language they are either
untranslated or only half translated. The owner redrew them in Russian;
do the same here.

HOW TO DRAW
-----------
  - Transparent background, letters only, no margins needed — I crop it
    myself.
  - NO anti-aliasing, hard pixels. Do not draw the edge: I add it by
    downscaling, the same way it was done for English and Russian.
  - Letter height ABOUT 22 PIXELS. Not two or three times the target
    size: at an integer multiple the edge does not form at all — this was
    checked by measurement.
  - Any dark colour is fine; I recolour it to the native ink (54,47,45)
    anyway.

I fit the width to the line myself. If a word turns out really long, I will
say so — the letters would come out lower than the native ones, and a
shorter word would be better.

TITLE SCREEN, separately
------------------------
File <language>/gfx/ui/main menu/titlemenu_ios.png, sheet 480x540. The
lettering at the bottom is drawn TWICE — edit both. It currently reads
«시작하세요.»; needed: 터치하세요

Unlike the menu labels, the title is drawn in HARD PIXELS, with no
anti-aliasing and no downscaling: every native title has exactly zero
half-tones. If you hand it in anti-aliased, I will harden it with a
threshold.
